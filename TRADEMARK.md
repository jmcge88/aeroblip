# Trademark policy

The software in this repository is free software under the
[GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). **The project's name, logo, product names and domain
names are not.**

Nothing in the AGPL grants rights to trademarks — see AGPL-3.0 section 7(e).
This page states plainly how the marks may and may not be used.

## The marks

"AeroBlip", the AeroBlip logo, and the `aeroblip.com` domain names
(collectively, "the Marks") are the property of the project's author.

## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your project is **built on**, **derived from**,
  **compatible with**, or **a fork of** AeroBlip.
- Use the Marks in ordinary descriptive prose: blog posts, reviews, comparisons,
  documentation, talks, academic work.
- Keep the Marks in an **unmodified** copy that you redistribute.
- Link to the project.

## What you may not do without written permission

- Use the Marks as the name of your fork, distribution, product, service or
  company, or as part of one (including "AeroBlip Pro", "AeroBlip Cloud",
  "AeroBlip-compatible" used as a product name rather than a description).
- Sell hardware, kits, assembled units or subscriptions **under the Marks**.
- Use the Marks or a confusingly similar name in a domain name, app store
  listing, social media handle, or marketplace listing.
- Use the Marks in a way that suggests your version is official, endorsed by,
  or affiliated with this project when it is not.
- Use the logo in modified form.

## If you fork it

The AGPL gives you the right to fork, modify, and even sell this software. To do
so you must **rebrand**: remove the Marks from the user interface, device
screens, documentation, packaging and listings, and pick your own name. The code
is yours to use under the AGPL; the identity is not.

This mirrors how many open-source projects separate code from brand, and it is
not a restriction on the software freedoms the AGPL grants you.

## Questions

If you would like to use the Marks in a way this page does not permit, ask.
Permission is often granted for reasonable uses.
